Warning Signs

Think you might have a leak?

If any of these sound familiar, it's worth getting checked before the damage becomes expensive.

An unexplained spike in your water bill

Damp patches, bubbling paint or mould

Running water sounds with every tap off

The water meter ticking over overnight

Cracked tiles or lifting floorboards

Warm or constantly wet spots on the floor

Water pressure that's dropped for no reason

Pooling water outside when it hasn't rained
