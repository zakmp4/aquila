Local Knowledge

Different suburbs,
different leaks.

A canal-front home in Broadbeach Waters doesn't fail the same way a slab-on-ground house in Varsity Lakes does. Neither behaves like an older timber home in Murwillumbah or a beachfront unit at Kingscliff.

Working the same stretch of coast every week means we know the local housing stock — and where to look first. That saves time on site, which saves you money.

  • Canal & waterfront homes

    Broadbeach Waters, Mermaid Waters and Banora Point, where salt and water tables complicate the picture.

  • Slab-on-ground estates

    Robina, Varsity Lakes and Elanora, where under-slab lines are the usual suspect.

  • Coastal units & strata

    Coolangatta, Tweed Heads and Kingscliff, where stacked bathrooms mean the leak is rarely in the unit that's wet.

  • Acreage & older homes

    Mudgeeraba, Tallebudgera, Terranora and Murwillumbah, with long buried service runs from meter to house.
