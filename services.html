Water Leak Detection

The core of what we do. Hidden leaks inside walls, under floors, in ceilings and beneath the ground can run for months before you see a stain. We use acoustic listening, thermal imaging, moisture mapping and pressure testing together — each one narrowing the search — until we can mark the exact spot with confidence.

  • Acoustic listening on pressurised lines
  • Thermal imaging of walls, floors and ceilings
  • Moisture mapping to trace water travel
  • Pressure testing to confirm the line at fault
  • Exact location marked on site
  • Findings explained in plain English

Underground Leaks

Buried service lines and water mains are the hardest leaks to find and the most expensive to guess at. Ground microphones and correlation equipment let us hear water escaping through soil, concrete and driveways, so the excavation is one small hole rather than a trench across your lawn.

  • Ground microphone surveys
  • Leak noise correlation across pipe runs
  • Pipe and service line tracing
  • Water meter isolation testing
  • Driveway, lawn and garden bed inspections
  • Excavation point marked and photographed

Slab Leak Detection

A leak under a concrete slab quietly undermines the thing your house is standing on. Warm patches on the floor, unexplained damp along a wall or a water bill that keeps climbing are the usual first signs. We locate slab leaks precisely so the repair is a targeted cut, not a floor-wide excavation.

  • Under-slab acoustic detection
  • Thermal scanning for hot water lines
  • Tracer gas testing where required
  • Depth and position estimates
  • Foundation moisture assessment
  • Repair access point marked out

Shower & Bathroom Leaks

Failed waterproofing, cracked grout, a hairline split in a waste pipe — bathroom leaks show up as swollen skirting, lifting floorboards or a damp patch in the room next door. We test the shower base, waste and supply lines separately to work out exactly which one is failing, usually without removing a single tile.

  • Shower base and waste flood testing
  • Waterproofing membrane assessment
  • Grout, silicone and seal inspection
  • Moisture readings in adjoining rooms
  • Supply line pressure checks
  • Tile-preserving diagnosis

Reports & Inspections

Insurers, real estate agents and body corporates all want the same thing: evidence. We document what we found, where we found it, and what the likely cause was — with photos, thermal images and clear conclusions — in a report you can hand straight to a claims assessor or a strata committee.

  • Written reports for insurance claims
  • Pre-purchase and pre-settlement inspections
  • Body corporate and strata reporting
  • Photographic and thermal evidence
  • Cause-of-damage assessment
  • Follow-up support for your plumber
