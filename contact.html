Before You Call

Quick answers

Turn off every tap and water-using appliance, then check your water meter. If the dial is still moving, water is going somewhere it shouldn't. Take a photo of the meter reading, wait an hour, photograph it again — that's genuinely useful evidence for us and for an insurer.

We quote up front before we come out, so you know the cost before anyone books a time. No hidden extras added afterwards.

Our specialty is detection — finding the leak precisely so the repair is small and targeted. We'll give you and your plumber everything needed to fix it properly, and we're happy to talk them through the findings.

It depends on the week, but we don't leave people waiting a fortnight. Call and we'll tell you the earliest realistic slot rather than an optimistic one.
