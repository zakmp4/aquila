Our Approach

Find it first.
Break things never.

Most water damage doesn't start with a burst pipe. It starts with a slow leak nobody can see — behind a wall, under a slab, beneath a shower base — quietly running up a water bill and softening the structure around it.

The old way of dealing with that was to start opening things up until the water showed itself. That's expensive, it's messy, and it's often wrong. We built Aquila around the opposite idea: use the technology, find the leak accurately, and only then decide what needs to be opened.

The result is a smaller repair, a faster fix and a homeowner who knows exactly what they're paying for.

The Toolkit

Four ways to see through a wall.

No single instrument finds every leak. Used together, they turn a whole-house mystery into a marked spot on the floor.

  • Acoustic Detection

    Ground microphones and correlators pick up the sound of escaping water through concrete, soil and cavity walls.

  • Thermal Imaging

    Temperature differences reveal where water is travelling behind tiles, plaster and under floors.

  • Moisture Mapping

    Meters trace the wettest path back toward the source instead of guessing from the stain.

  • Pressure & Tracer Gas

    Isolating and pressurising lines confirms which pipe is failing, and tracer gas finds the smallest of them.
